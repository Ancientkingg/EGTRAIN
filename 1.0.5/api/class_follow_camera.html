Detailed Description

Keeps the centre of a NetworkView on a target scene position. It owns neither a train nor a snapshot: the caller passes positions in and the controller moves the view.

Every target moves the view to it at once, to a fraction of a pixel. The window passes the position at which a followed train is drawn at every step of its motion, so the view moves with the train in the same frame. The first target after stop(), a snap and a target further away than 1.5 times the larger side of the visible scene are a cut, which also ends a catch-up. Where the edge of the scene holds an axis of the view, the view stays at the edge on that axis.

While the user moves the view (NetworkView::interacting()), holding a drag, scrolling or pinching, the controller does not move it: it keeps the target and ends a catch-up, and a snap meanwhile cuts at the first target after. It adopts the centre after a pan, a zoom or a resize by the user, and also after a drag in which the view did not move. Otherwise the first target after the user has finished starts a catch-up: the view keeps the distance from the target that the user left and closes it over catchUpMs, easing in and out, with a timer of its own so that a catch-up also ends while the target stands still. Without a new target, as in a paused run, the view stays where the user left it.
