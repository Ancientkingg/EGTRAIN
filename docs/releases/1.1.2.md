# Network pictures and passenger rules

EGTRAIN 1.1.2 saves the network as a picture, settles three routing rules for passengers and measures the delay of a replanned journey against its first plan. Runs with many waiting passengers take less time per step, and the pictures in the guides show the new map key.

- Network picture: File > Export Network Image... saves the whole network as a PNG or SVG picture, as the view shows it at Fit with the layers that are on. It works before, during and after a run and leaves the zoom of the window as it is.
- Routing: when two trains arrive at the same time with the same number of changes, a passenger takes the one that leaves first. A passenger boards a train at their platform that reaches their destination directly and no later than their plan. The walk at a change is a case setting in Case Settings, 30 s per platform number of difference by default.
- Delay: a passenger who misses a connection and is planned again gets the delay against the arrival of their first plan. This changes the Delay column of `PassengerJourneys.txt`, MeanDelay in `ODSummary.txt` and `JourneyDelays.txt` for replanned journeys.
- Speed: the station counts and platform lists no longer walk every waiting passenger at each step. At 200,000 waiting passengers the station counts take 120 µs per step instead of 7.6 ms. `PassengerJourneys.txt` is written row by row, with the same content.
- Smaller changes: the station tooltip and a passenger's text name a platform as "Platform 2" or "platform 2", the Demand CSV export proposes the slice id as the file name, and ids with a tab or a line break are rejected because the result files cannot hold them.

Release 1.1.1 and older open a case with the new walking setting without a message, use their old walking rule and leave the key out when they save it.
