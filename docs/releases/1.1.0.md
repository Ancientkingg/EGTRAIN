# The Passengers Update

EGTRAIN 1.1.0 simulates passengers. A case can hold origin-destination demand per time slice, and a run turns it into passengers who ride the trains, change between them and wait on the platforms. The network shows the crowding at each station, and Run Results shows the load of every train.

- Passenger demand: a case holds demand per time slice in `passengers.json`, edited on the Demand tab of the Passengers dock. A run turns the slices into passengers, who are routed on the live timetable, change trains within the transfer limit and board at the platform of their next train.
- Passenger settings: train units have a passenger capacity, and Case Settings has the transfer limit and the dwell parameters. Journey windows are entered as times of day. Validation warns about journeys that cannot be made in their window and checks demand slices, capacities and dwell coefficients.
- Passenger results: Run Results shows the load of each train with its peak and average, and a run writes result files per journey, per stop and per station pair.
- Network view: waiting passengers show as crowding gauges on the platforms and as counts at the stations. A station opens a card with its passengers, and a right-click on a platform bar or a count opens the station menu.
- Simulation: a virtually coupled follower holds the speed of its leader and never runs into it, a short leader at signalling level 4 no longer takes its follower as its leader, and a train that starts at its first stop blocks the sections under it at levels 0 and 2.
- Editor: a case above the passenger limit can be edited down on the Demand tab, six smaller Demand tab defects are fixed, track layout cells are guarded, and a new station gets an id without spaces.

Release 1.0.9 and older open a case with the new passenger keys without a message, run it without the demand, and leave the keys out when they save it. Keep a copy of such a case before you save it with an older release.
