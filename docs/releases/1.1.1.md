# A tidier map key

EGTRAIN 1.1.1 gives the map key at the bottom of the Case and Layers dock the look of the Layers section above it. Its rows take less room and its speed rows fit on one line.

- The Map key heading has the type, colour and spacing of the Layers heading, with a small arrow that shows whether the key is open.
- The heading and the rows share one left edge, and every symbol sits in the same column.
- The speed rows read "Track 200+ km/h" and "Track 120+ km/h". The labels use the width of the dock and wrap only in a narrow one.
- The rows sit closer together, so the open key is shorter.

The pictures in the guides still show the old map key. A later release retakes them.
