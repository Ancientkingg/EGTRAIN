Report a problem

Report a problem with EGTRAIN, or ask a question about it, by email to egtrain@samuelbruin.com. The link opens a new email with a subject and a list of what to include.

What to include

  • the version of EGTRAIN, which Help > About EGTRAIN shows (on macOS the entry is in the menu EGTRAIN);
  • your system and its version, such as macOS 26 or Windows 11;
  • the case, and the steps that lead to the problem;
  • what you expected and what happened instead, with the exact text of any message.

What should I attach to a bug report? in the user guides says where to find each of them, and which log lines help.

A case file, a log or a picture can hold data that you do not want to share: attach one only when the problem needs it, and remove what you do not want to share.

Documentation

The user guides describe installing EGTRAIN, running and editing cases, and the results. Common questions answers the questions that come up most. EGTRAIN also holds the user guides of its own version: choose Help > EGTRAIN Manual or press F1.

The documentation of every version is listed on the home page.
