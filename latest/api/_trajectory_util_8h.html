Detailed Description

Helpers for the position series of a train. A series has one sample per step of the run, its index is the step in seconds from the start of the run, and a position is the head position in metres along the route. -9999 marks a sample without a position, such as after the train has left.

The cells of trajectoryExportCells() moved in time: one cell per step from outputFirst to outputLast, both included, in which the sample activeFirst falls on departureTime. Steps are seconds from the start of the run. A cell without a valid sample is -9999. Returns an empty vector when outputFirst is after outputLast, and only -9999 when activeFirst is negative.
