Detailed Description

The name of a station on the network canvas: white text in a halo of HaloPixels logical pixels in canvasColor(), a child item stacked behind it, so that a track, a pin or a train under the name does not cross its letters. It is stacked over everything else on the canvas except the boxes that a click opens, and it takes no click. The text and the font stay as constructed.

◆ haloItem()

QGraphicsItem * StationNameItem::haloItem ( ) const
inline

The halo, a child item behind the name, drawn on every paint without a cached picture. While Qt draws the letters from its glyph cache, it is the letters drawn sixteen times around their place, in eight directions at half and at all of HaloPixels on screen; for larger letters it is a stroke along their outlines that reaches as far past them. It is at most as wide as at the smallest size of the name on screen, StationOverlayItem::MinNamePixels.
