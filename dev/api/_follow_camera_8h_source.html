13// Moves the centre of a NetworkView towards a target scene position. It owns neither a
14// train nor a snapshot: the caller passes positions in and the controller moves the view.
15//
16// A glide follows the target with a timer of its own. The view moves at a constant speed and
17// reaches the newest target one estimated update interval after that target arrived, and never
18// sooner than two timer steps. It is about one update behind a train that moves at constant
19// speed, and moves at one speed between updates and across them. The distance to the target is
20// capped to a fraction of the visible scene, and the timer stops when the target is reached or
21// the view cannot get closer to it. A snap moves the view at once.
22//
23// The controller reads the real centre back after every move to detect a clamped axis, and
24// leaves a clamped axis out of the distance, the step and the lag cap of the free axis. It
25// adopts the centre after a pan, a zoom or a resize by the user as the new starting point, so
26// the next glide returns from there.
