◆ paintStationMark()

void paintStationMark ( QPainter * painter,
const QRectF & frame,
StationClass cls,
const QColor & fill,
const QColor & halo,
qreal haloWidth )

Paints the mark of a station class, a map pin with its tip on the bottom centre of frame: a circle of radius 0.3 k of the side of frame whose centre is 0.78 k of it above the tip, and the two tangents from the tip to the circle. k is 1 for a station, a pin in fill with a disc of 0.4 of its radius in the halo colour at the centre of the circle; 0.8 for a halt, a pin in the halo colour with an edge of 0.8 haloWidth and a disc of 0.38 of its radius in fill; and 1.15 for a major station, a blue (80, 120, 210) pin with an edge of 0.6 haloWidth and a disc of 0.42 of its radius in fill. A halo of haloWidth, in the units of the painter, surrounds the mark.
