StopDraftCheck validateStopDraft ( const SceneModel & model,

const SceneStop & draft,
const StopDraftFields & fields )

Checks a stop of the stop dialog in this order: an added stop at a position that offers no station, the planned arrival and departure, which must parse when their box is checked and have text, the dwell, a finite number of 0 or more, the station, and a platform that is ambiguous on the route when the stop is added or its station or platform differs from the stop at position of service. An added stop is then inserted with insertSceneStop() into a copy of service, and a refusal is the last problem. draft holds the station and the platform, and its planned times are kept where the text of a field still shows them. service is not changed.
