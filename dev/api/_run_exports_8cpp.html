◆ compressedBlockingTimeDiagramData()

std::optional< BlockingTimeDiagramData > compressedBlockingTimeDiagramData ( const CompletedRunData & data,
const CapacityAnalysisScope & scope,
const CapacityAnalysisResult & result )

The compressed blocking-time diagram of a capacity result, on the route and the blocks of its scope: the compressed occupations of result as envelopes, and the recorded samples of each train of the result that lie inside them, moved in time as its occupations are (compressed minus profile reference). An envelope that the route of its train does not project lies on the blocks of the scope that share a resource with it, from the first to the last. With scope.repeatFirstTrain the first train one period later is a repeated train of the first train, whose samples move by its compressed reference minus the profile reference of the first train. It has no planned references. The time range spans the envelopes with a margin of 5 % of their span, at least 1 s, and starts below 0 s only when an envelope does; it is 0 s to 1 s without an envelope. Empty when the scope has no route.

◆ delayDiagramData()

std::optional< DelayDiagramData > delayDiagramData ( const CompletedRunData & data,
int referenceIndex )

The arrival delay of each call of each train at a station of the reference route with the given index, at the position of that station on the route. A call is at the station nodes of the route whose station name is the station of the call (TimetableResultRow::stationId holds the name). When the route passes the station at more than one position, the call takes the first that the train reaches at or after its previous call at a station of the route, moving as between its last two such calls that lie apart, or before those as from its first to its last call at a station that the route passes once. Otherwise it takes the first of them on the route. Empty when the route does not exist or has no nodes.
