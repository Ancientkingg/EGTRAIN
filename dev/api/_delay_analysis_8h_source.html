8// Delay analysis of completed runs, computed from the values of RunResults.h.
9//
10// Every time is in seconds and every share is in percent. A value that does not
11// exist is an unavailable RunResultValue and never 0. The functions read no
12// other state and change none.
13//
14// Definitions:
15// - An event is the arrival or the departure of one call of an occurrence. A
16// pass-through is not a call and has no event.
17// - The planned lateness of an event is its simulated time minus its planned
18// time, signed: negative is early, 0 is on time, positive is late. It is
19// stored without a tolerance. The planned time is the authored one: the
20// planned departure of the stop that an entrance delay moved is taken back
21// by the entrance delay, so that stop's departure shows the entrance delay.
22// - The entrance delay of an occurrence is the primary delay. It is an input
23// of the scenario, never inferred from a run.
24// - The additional delay of an event is the simulated time in the scenario run
25// minus the simulated time of the same occurrence and call in the baseline
26// run.
27// - The secondary delay of an occurrence is its additional delay at the final
28// arrival minus the entrance delay, when the stop that the entrance delay
29// moved comes before the final call. It is a difference of two numbers and
30// says nothing about a cause.
31// - Direct evidence is the recording of a direct incident effect on the
32// occurrence in the scenario run. An occurrence without evidence is not
33// shown to be delayed by propagation.
