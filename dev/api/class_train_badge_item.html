Detailed Description

The badge of a train, placed at the centre of its bodies. It ignores the transformation of the view, so its geometry is in logical pixels from its position. It keeps the identifier, the speed, the kind and the direction of the train and works out its layout, but it paints nothing, takes no click and shows no tooltip: the canvas shows the bodies of the train alone.
