Detailed Description

The Trains section of a diagram window: the train filter and the colour key in one list. Each row has a check box that shows or hides the train, a line sample in its colour and its id. More than six trains in six services or fewer are grouped by service, with a group row whose check box shows and hides the trains of the group and is partly checked while some of them are shown, and whose line sample has the colour that its shown trains share, or while none is shown the colour that all its trains share; other lists are flat. The selected train comes first and is highlighted, then the shown trains, then the hidden ones, and groups follow the same order; while the pointer is over the list the rows keep their places, and they move once it leaves, also when the list keeps the focus. A change made with the keyboard while the pointer is elsewhere moves them once the focus leaves. Above 6 trains All and None, at their own width, show or hide the trains of the rows in the list, and above 12 a search field beside them narrows the rows; a group then counts and acts on its trains that the search shows, and Escape in the field clears it. The count of shown trains, such as "2 of 2", is a label that the panel puts on the heading row of the section.
