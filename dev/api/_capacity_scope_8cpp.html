◆ capacityTrainOrder()

std::vector< CapacityAnalysisTrain > capacityTrainOrder ( std::vector< CapacityAnalysisTrain > trains)

The order in which the trains use the blocks they share, for trains in run order. Of two trains that share a resource, the leader is the one whose occupation starts first in the pair of shared occupations, one of each, with the earliest start; when both start together, the one with the earlier profile reference, then the earlier in run order. Each train comes after its leaders; of the trains that may come next, the one with the earliest profile reference, then the earlier in run order. When none may come next, because the leaders of the trains left form cycles, that rule picks among the trains whose unplaced leaders can all be reached from them through the trains they lead: the trains of a cycle that no other train left leads.

◆ chooseCapacityAnalysisScope()

bool chooseCapacityAnalysisScope ( QWidget * parent,
const CompletedRunData & run,
CapacityAnalysisScope & scope )

Asks for a route, a range of its blocks, a period, the trains to analyse in order and the train that closes the cycle: the first train one period later or a checked train after the first. The list holds the trains of the range (capacityInputsFrom) in the order of capacityTrainOrder, all checked, and a new route or range lists them again, with the trains that were unchecked still unchecked. It opens on the scope when the run has its route: its range, the trains it names checked and first in their order, the other trains of the range unchecked, and its cycle-closing train. Else it opens on firstCapacityScopeWithPairFrom. On accept the scope holds the choice. False when the dialog is cancelled or the run has no route.
