Detailed Description

One per-step series of a train, the head position or the speed, for every step that the vector of the train had. Five regions follow each other: a value that repeats at the start, the samples that are kept here, the samples shared with the completed run, the samples kept here after those, and a value that repeats to the end. Every sample is reproduced bit for bit.
