◆ chooseBlockingTimeScope()

bool chooseBlockingTimeScope ( QWidget * parent,
const BlockingTimeScopeChoices & choices,
BlockingTimeScope & scope,
const ScopeStretchObserver & observer = {} )

Asks for a route, a stretch of it (ScopeStretchChoice) and an interval as clock times of the run, from and to a second of the run. It opens on the route and blocks of the scope when the choices have its route, else on all routes, and on its interval. On accept the scope holds the choice and no train list, so it takes every train that occupies a block of the stretch, whatever route the train runs on. False when the dialog is cancelled or the interval is empty.

◆ routeStationsFrom()

std::vector< RouteStation > routeStationsFrom ( const std::vector< BlockSectionInput > & blocks,
const RouteDiagramPath & path )

The stations that the path passes, in its order, each with the blocks around it. A node of a station lies on a block when its position on the route lies between the ends of the block, ends included. Nodes of one station that follow each other on the path, with no node of another station between them, are one visit; a station visited again later is listed again. A station whose nodes lie on no block is left out.

The stations whose stretch (stationStretchBlocks) is the smallest that holds the blocks first to last: the stretch of those blocks itself when the stations give it, then the stations nearest the blocks. Of stretches of one length, the earliest From station and the latest To station. Empty when the route has no station or no stretch holds the blocks.

◆ stationStretchBlocks()

std::pair< int, int > stationStretchBlocks ( const BlockingTimeScopeChoices::Route & route,
int from,
int to )

The blocks, from 0 in route order, of the stretch from station from to station to of the route, positions in stations in either order: every block that lies at least partly between the two stations (from the first node of the one to the last node of the other), where the first and the last station of the route stand for its ends. From a station to itself, the blocks that hold it. The first and the last block of the stretch; the whole route when it has no station.
