◆ buildSceneComposition()

bool buildSceneComposition ( const SceneModel & scene,
const std::string & compositionId,
SceneCompositionRuntime & result,
std::string & diagnostic )

Resolves the ordered, possibly repeated, unit references of a composition. A single unit is copied. Two or more are combined: traction unit masses, wagon counts and lengths add up, the wagon mass becomes the mean per wagon, the resistance coefficient the mass-weighted mean, the lowest speed, deceleration and jerk apply, the first frontal area is kept, and the traction curves add up per speed band. Returns false and sets diagnostic on failure.
