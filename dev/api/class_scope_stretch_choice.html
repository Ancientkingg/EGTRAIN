Detailed Description

The rows Route / corridor, Stretch, From and To of the two scope dialogs, and what they choose. The route list names each route by blockingTimeScopeRouteText. Stretch chooses between Stations and Blocks: From station and To station list the stations of the route (stationStretchBlocks gives their blocks), From block and To block list its blocks as "1: B0 0.000 to 0.230 km" with the short block id as tooltip. Stations is offered for a route with two stations or more. A From entry after the To entry moves the To entry to it, and a To entry before the From entry moves the From entry. A new route takes all of its blocks, from its first to its last station with Stations. Blocks shows the blocks that the stations chose, and Stations shows the stations that stationsHoldingBlocks gives for the blocks.

void ScopeStretchChoice::choose ( int index,
const std::vector< int > & indices )

Chooses the route at index with its blocks indices (positions in either order, the range from the lowest to the highest), or all its blocks when there are none: as stations when the stations of the route give that stretch, else as blocks. An index of no route chooses the first entry of the route list.

The pointer or the arrow keys are on an entry of the open route, From or To list: the chosen route and stretch as stretchChanged gives them, and the section ids of the entry: all blocks of a route, the blocks that hold a station (RouteStation firstBlock to lastBlock) or the one block; none for "All routes". A list that closes sends the chosen route and stretch with no entry.
