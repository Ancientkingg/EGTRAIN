Detailed Description

One kind of mark in the key of a chart, such as "Blocking time" or "Planned", with the pen and brush that draw it.

Enumerator
Line 

A short horizontal line in the pen, with a dot in its middle when marker is set.

Box 

A box filled with the brush and outlined with the pen.

Marker 

A dot filled with the brush and outlined with the pen.

Tick 

A short vertical line in the pen, such as a boundary or a dwell.

Cell 

A cell of the section strip: background outlined a shade darker, then the brush, and a bar in the pen colour along its top when the pen draws
