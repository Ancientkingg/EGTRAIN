38// Undo and Redo as a position in a list of whole-scene snapshots. The entry at the position is
39// the live scene. Recording an edit appends a copy and drops the entries after the position, so
40// an edit after Undo abandons the Redo branch. When either limit is exceeded, the oldest
41// entries are dropped; the entry at the position never is.
42//
43// One position can be marked as saved. The scene is clean exactly when the position is the saved
44// one. Dropping the saved entry, by an edit after undoing past it or by a limit, leaves no saved
45// position, and the scene stays dirty until the next save.
46//
47// A history always has a root. A new history holds an empty scene as its root until reset. The
48// class has no Qt dependency and is used by one thread at a time; the scenes that it returns
49// are immutable and may be read from other threads.
50class SceneHistory {
51public:
52 explicit SceneHistory(SceneHistoryLimits limits = {});
