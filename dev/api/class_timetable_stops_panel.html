Detailed Description

The timetable of one service: its stops in order, with the station, the platform, the dwell and the planned times, and the buttons that add, remove and move them. A stop is added or edited in a dialog on a copy and enters the service only when the stop still fits the route. The service is the one whose id the panel is given. The five columns fill the width of the table down to their minimum widths: the platform and the times take what their header and a typical value need and the station takes the rest. A table narrower than that shortens the columns, and long text is elided. The minimum dwell is in seconds, and the planned arrival and departure are seconds from the base time, shown either as such or as clock times; a planned time can be absent.
