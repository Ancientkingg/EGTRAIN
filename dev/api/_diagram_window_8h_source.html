39// Reusable non-modal window that shows a chart with a train visibility
40// dropdown, hover and click identification, rubber-band zoom with reset,
41// PNG export, and optional CSV export.
42//
43// Series are grouped by the dynamic property "trainId" when the caller sets it
44// (the same train can own several series, such as planned and simulated lines);
45// series without that property are grouped by their own name.
46// Optional inspection metadata: "inspectionPoints" is a QStringList with one
47// plain-text context entry per appended point (including empty entries).
48// "inspectionInterval" is plain-text context shared by a line segment;
49// "inspectionFilled" explicitly opts a closed QLineSeries into interior hits.
50// A line hit always reports its nearest actual plotted sample, not an
