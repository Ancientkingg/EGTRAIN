The axes of a chart along a reference route: the distance along the bottom, spanning the route exactly, and the stations above the plot, both attached to every series. Gives the station axis and its ticks for DiagramWindow::setStationAxis, or null when the route has no station. Each of callPositions, the route distances of the calls that the chart shows, counts as a call at the station nearest to it.

◆ buildRouteDiagramChart()

RouteChart buildRouteDiagramChart ( const RouteDiagramData & diagram,

double horizonSeconds )

The route diagram: the lines of diagram over the distance along its reference route and the time of the run. The time axis runs from 0 to horizonSeconds, and to at least 1 s. calls gives the stops of the trains that count at each station, so that the busiest stations are named first; a train path chart passes the timetable graph of its reference route, a timetable graph itself.
