Detailed Description

The overlay of a station at its anchor in the scene. It ignores the transformation of the view, so its own geometry is in logical pixels around the anchor. On the network canvas it is a scene decoration: it draws nothing itself and keeps the scene-space picture and name of the station readable (attachArtwork()). Otherwise it draws its own mark and label.

◆ applyViewScale()

void StationOverlayItem::applyViewScale ( qreal viewScale,
bool nearLook = false )

Scales the attached items for the view scale (logical pixels per scene unit). A station mark stands with the bottom centre of its frame on the top edge of the convex hull of its node centres, or on its anchor without them, at the x of the centre of the footprint of its squares (stationFootprintRect()): a station or major station on the top centre of their box for a single centre, a row or a stack, a halt on its first square, and a diagonal station on its own area. It stands there in both looks and draws nothing in the near look, which a mark without node centres does not take: it keeps its pin as the only thing to see and click, and there the pin and the name keep their smallest size on screen, MinPictogramPixels and MinNamePixels, at every zoom. Its name sits under the footprint in both looks, so that mark and name share their x. The area of the station is shown while the mark is visible: in the far look the hull of its node centres widened by AreaMarginPixels on screen, and in the near look the hull of its node centres and platform bars widened by as much. A plain picture keeps the far look and, like its name, its artwork point.

◆ attachArtwork()

void StationOverlayItem::attachArtwork ( QGraphicsPixmapItem * picture,
QGraphicsTextItem * name,
int platformCount )

Links the scene-space pictogram and name of this station. The items stay owned by the scene; both are enlarged about their anchor (bottom centre of the pictogram, top centre of the name) through their item transform, which stays the identity at their scene size. For a station mark the transform also moves these anchors to the station nodes (applyViewScale()). Attach the items to their parents before the first applyViewScale: the transform moves scenePos() of an enlarged item. More platforms rank a station higher when names collide. For a StationMarkItem that is in a scene it also adds the area of the station to the scene, a path item at AreaZValue in stationAreaColor() that takes no mouse input.

◆ resolveNameCollisions()

void StationOverlayItem::resolveNameCollisions ( const QList< StationOverlayItem * > & stations,
const QTransform & sceneToViewport )
static

Places the names of station marks and hides the attached names that overlap, on screen, the name of a higher-priority station or that cover part of the drawn pictogram of any other station: a visible plain picture, the pictogram rect of a visible mark that draws its pin, and the footprint of the squares of a visible mark in the near look. A name goes under the footprint of the squares of its station, or, where its text there crosses a visible track line or the pictogram of another station, shown or hidden, to the first of these spots that crosses neither: above the pin, right of the footprint, left of it, and above a frame on the top of the box of the node centres at the x of the footprint; without one it stays under the footprint. A mark that draws nothing in the near look has no pin and no frame there, so its name goes above the footprint instead of above the pin and has no last spot. A name is placed again only after applyViewScale() moved it. Selected and followed stations keep their name. Pictograms stay visible. sceneToViewport is the viewport transform of the view.
