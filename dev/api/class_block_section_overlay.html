Detailed Description

One chart-owned item, outside the train series/filter identity space. Geometry is mapped from the live distance axis, which may be the position axis; the height of the strip stays in chart pixels. It draws the strip of the sections above the plot and the break marks of the position axis on the bottom edge of the plot over the series, and the boundaries of the sections in the plot under the series, in a layer of its own. The strip is one row of cells without text, one cell for each section with a usable extent.

◆ inspect()

QString BlockSectionOverlay::inspect ( const QPointF & point,
bool headerOnly ) const

The hover at point in chart pixels: a break mark, the section of the cell of the strip under the pointer, or the nearest within 3 px, and without headerOnly the sections of the boundaries in the plot. A section is two lines: its short id (shortBlockId) and its geo extent, such as "Position 21.010 to 21.450 km". Coincident sections are all listed. Empty where the overlay shows nothing.
