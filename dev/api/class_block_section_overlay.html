Detailed Description

One chart-owned item, outside the train series/filter identity space. Geometry is mapped from the live distance axis, which may be the position axis; font sizes and the strip stay in chart pixels. It draws the strip of the sections above the plot and the break marks of the position axis on the bottom edge of the plot over the series, and the boundaries of the sections in the plot under the series, in a layer of its own.
