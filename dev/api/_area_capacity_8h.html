Enumeration Type Documentation

◆ AreaCapacityNoteKind

enum class AreaCapacityNoteKind
strong

What a note reports. Each kind names the ids that it fills in AreaCapacityNote; the other ids are empty.

Enumerator
EmptyScope 

No resource id is left. Nothing else is computed.

InvalidPeriod 

The period is not finite or not above 0. Every percentage is -1.0.

DuplicateIdentity 

trainId repeats the runtimeId of an earlier entry of selected or otherTraffic. The repeating entry is ignored.

NoSelectedTraffic 

No selected train is left.

IncompleteOccupation 

trainId has an occupation on resourceId that fails validBlockingTimeDiagramInput. It is used nowhere else.

RepeatedResource 

trainId has two or more usable occupations on resourceId.

OpposingTrain 

trainId has another reversedDirection than the first selected train. It is left out of the chain and still counts in the rows and the envelope.

InvalidReference 

trainId, a train of the chain, has a profile reference that is not finite or is below 0.

FewerThanTwoTrains 

The chain holds exactly one train.

NoSharedResource 

The adjacent chain trains trainId and otherTrainId share no resource. Not reported when one of them has an invalid reference.

ChainConflict 

The chain holds at least two trains and its compressed occupations overlap.

UnselectedTraffic 

trainId is in otherTraffic and has an occupation in the area, usable or not.
