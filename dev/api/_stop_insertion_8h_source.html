54// Inserts `stop` at `insertIndex` only when the new stop resolves and every stop
55// that resolved before still resolves at the same route visit. Otherwise
56// `service` is left unchanged and the result names the stops concerned. No field

59//
60// Callers choose positions and stations with sceneStopInsertionWindow. This
