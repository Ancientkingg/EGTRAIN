Detailed Description

Reusable non-modal window that shows a chart in a ResultFrame: a header row with the context and the zoom, fit and panel buttons over the chart, and a panel beside it with the sections Trains (the train filter and colour key in one list, a TrainKeyList), Key (the kinds of mark in view, a DiagramKey), Selected and Export, and a readout of the value under the pointer. The chart has hover and click identification and rubber-band zoom, and exports a PNG and, optionally, a CSV.

The Key lists each kind of mark that a shown series draws, with the pen and brush of the series: the "keyKind" property of a series names its kind, and an empty one leaves the series out; without it the "layer" property names the kind, else the series is Planned or Simulated. A closed outline whose "inspectionFilled" property is set is a box, filled with the brush of an area series of the same kind; another series is a line in the text colour, since its colour belongs to its train, with a dot when it shows its points, and upright when its "keyUpright" property is true, as for a dwell. The "keyMarker" property of a series that shows its points names a kind of its own for its points, such as "Stop". The chart draws the points of a planned series as open circles and those of another series as dots, and the Key draws them alike: a planned line with an open circle, a kind of points as a dot while a series that is not planned has them. Simulated is left out while it is the only kind of line. The section strip adds Station section, Switch section and Block boundary. The Key follows the trains that are shown. A box takes the pen of the "keyPen" property of its outline when the outline has one, so that it looks alike for every train. A chart of rolling stock input lists its series by name, in their own colours.

Series are grouped by the dynamic property "trainId" when the caller sets it (the same train can own several series, such as planned and simulated lines); series without that property are grouped by their own name. Optional inspection metadata: "inspectionPoints" is a QStringList with one plain-text context entry per appended point (including empty entries). "inspectionInterval" is plain-text context shared by a line segment; "inspectionFilled" explicitly opts a closed QLineSeries into interior hits, and "inspectionTitle" is the first line of such a box, such as "IC1723-2 on 12-B0". Inside a box a click selects a line only within 4 px of it, and otherwise the box. A line hit always reports its nearest actual plotted sample, not an interpolated value at the cursor.

One colour rule paints the trains, by the trains that the Trains list shows (trainColourPlan): a colour of the palette for each train while six trains or fewer are shown, else one for each service while those trains run in six services or fewer (setTrainServices), else grey with the selected train in the first colour of the palette. The colour of a service in the case wins over the palette (setTrainColors). Showing, hiding and selecting trains apply the rule again and keep which trains are checked. A series takes the colour of its train in its pen, and an area series also in its brush, with the alpha of the brush kept; widths and dash patterns stay as the chart builder set them. A series whose "ownColour" property is true keeps the colours of its builder, as do the series of a chart of rolling stock input. The line sample of a train in the Trains list has its colour, and that of a hidden train is light grey. The selected train is drawn over the other trains.

A chart without series shows the text of setEmptyText in the middle of its plot, and the panel leaves out the sections that have nothing to show, Trains included.

void DiagramWindow::setBlockSections ( std::vector< BlockSectionExtent > sections,

Draws the block sections of the reference route over the chart, with positions in km. Set after setChart. A chart-owned physical layer, not train series, with its entries in the Key. Chart replacement clears it. The strip above the plot names each section by its short id (shortBlockId), centred in its cell, in one row when every id fits at the full view of the chart and otherwise in two, where an id that collides in the first row tries the second. When the ids in route order leave one out, those of critical and conflict sections, the first and last section, then switch and station sections are placed first. A switch section is hatched, and marks, one per section or empty, add a dark top bar for a station, an amber bar for a critical block and a red one for a conflict. The hover of a cell or a boundary gives the id, the label from labels, the kind, "Section N of M" with M routeSectionCount (the number of sections when 0), the geo extent and the route distance.

◆ setChart()

void DiagramWindow::setChart ( QChart * chart)

Shows the chart and takes ownership of it. The chart loses its title, since the window title and the exported figure name the chart. Numeric axes that keep the ticks Qt gives them get ticks on round values, and their range widens to the first round tick past each end; a range that starts at zero keeps that start. An axis whose "exactRange" property is true keeps its range and still gets round ticks. A series is planned when its "planned" property is true or its name holds "planned"; a planned series is drawn dashed. A line narrower than 2 px is drawn 2 px wide, and the outline of a box ("inspectionFilled") keeps its width. A new chart ends the selection.

const QString & suggestedFileName,
const QStringList & trainIds = {} )

Supply raw source data for CSV export. The provider receives the ids of the trains currently visible so it can export only what the user is looking at. trainIds adds to the train filter the trains that have CSV rows but no plotted series. An empty return means there is nothing to export. The CSV button is shown only while a provider is set. A save names the file in the status bar of the main window that is the parent of this window. The PNG export suggests the name of setFigureFileName, else suggestedFileName with the suffix png, or diagram.png without a name. After an export the window is raised and active again, saved or cancelled (KeepInFront).

◆ setPositionAxis()

void DiagramWindow::setPositionAxis ( PositionScale scale)

Labels the bottom axis, a route distance in km, as the geo X of scale, titled "Position (km)": labels on round geo X values at their route distance, which count down where the route runs against the scene coordinate, and a break mark on the bottom edge of the plot at each jump of the scale, whose hover gives both geo X. The plot keeps the route distance. The labels follow every zoom, pan, fit and resize, and lie at least 60 px apart. The readout and the tooltip of a sample give the geo X. Set after setChart, not with setTimeAxisX; an empty scale restores the numeric axis. The numeric axis is hidden while the position axis takes its place, as with setTimeAxisY.

◆ setStationAxis()

void DiagramWindow::setStationAxis ( QCategoryAxis * axis,
std::vector< StationTick > stations )

Set after setChart. axis is a category axis of the chart that runs along the distance axis and has its own range. The window gives it the stations that are in its range and whose labels fit above the plot side by side, each with the longest spelling that fits, so the chart never has to shorten a label. A resize or a zoom chooses again. The labels are plain text; the chart reads them as rich text, so they are escaped. When the distance axis has the dynamic property "readoutStation", the readout names by its shortest spelling the station at the nearest position where a shown series has a sample, as in "2.5 min at Treviglio", and gives only the value of the left axis when no shown series has one.

◆ setTimeAxisY()

void DiagramWindow::setTimeAxisY ( bool on,
long long startOffsetSeconds = 0 )

Labels the Y axis as clock time, titled "Time (hh:mm)": the axis value in seconds plus startOffsetSeconds, the clock time of second 0 in seconds since midnight, with ticks on round clock times and one at second 0 while it is in range. Ticks on whole minutes read HH:MM while the ticks are at least a minute apart; a tick off a whole minute, such as second 0 at 07:10:40, and every tick of a shorter step read HH:MM:SS, so no two ticks read the same. False restores numeric labels. Set before or after setChart. Bounds and reversal come from the caller, without the round widening of setChart; reset restores those bounds. The numeric axis is hidden while the clock axis takes its place, so that the chart draws one axis on that side. Tooltips and the readout keep HH:MM:SS.
