const SceneSectionLabels & labels,

const QString & name,
QWidget * parent )

The tables of a chain of trains, which the Capacity window shows in tabs and Capacity by area on its Chain tab: Pairs, one row for each pair of adjacent trains, Compression, one for each train, and Critical blocks, one for each place where two trains touch after the compression. noun names what the trains occupy, "block" or "resource", in the headers Governing, Leader and Follower block and in the title Critical blocks. The tables are named name + "Pairs", "Compression" and "Critical". A cell names a block by its short id with its label in labels as its tooltip, and a cell that names a train has its reference time as its tooltip.
