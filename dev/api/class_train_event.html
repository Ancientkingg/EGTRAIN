Detailed Description

A record of a train at a place and time. It serves as the passage of a train at an infrastructure element (InfraEvent), as a timetable point of a train (Train::TimetablePoints) and as the train data of a movement authority. Times are seconds from the start of the run, positions metres and speeds m/s. Numbers are -10000 and strings "None" until set, and TrainSpeed is -1.
