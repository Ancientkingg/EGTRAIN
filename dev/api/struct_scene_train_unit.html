◆ tractionCurve

std::vector<std::array<double, 5> > SceneTrainUnit::tractionCurve

Rows of [v_lower, v_upper, c0, c1, c2]: the tractive effort in N is c0 + c1 v + c2 v² for a speed v from v_lower up to v_upper. The simulation compares the bounds with speeds in m/s.
