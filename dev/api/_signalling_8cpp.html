◆ activateBlocksWithSwitchesDiv()

void activateBlocksWithSwitchesDiv ( SimulationRun & run,
const Section & BS,
int TrackLineIDPrevBS,
double S )

For a train with its tail at route position S (m) on the level 3 or 4 section BS with a diverging switch: puts the block sections named in the ID of BS in run.blocksOccupied: the first while S is before the switch, both while it is on the switch, and the second after it, when both also go to run.blocksConnected. The two swap when TrackLineIDPrevBS is the track line of the second.
