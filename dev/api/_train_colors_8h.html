◆ styleBlockingTime()

void styleBlockingTime ( QAreaSeries * fill,
QLineSeries * outline,
BlockingTimeKind kind )

Gives the fill and the closed outline of a blocking time the look of its kind and its kind in the Key (SeriesMeta::keyKind), on top of the description that they have. An ordinary blocking time has a neutral grey fill and an outline 1.4 px wide that the diagram window paints in the colour of the train; the Key shows its outline neutral grey. A critical block is amber with a 45 degree hatch and an outline of amber brown, and a conflict is #9e1b1b with a cross hatch and an outline of that red; both keep their colours (SeriesMeta::ownColour). The pen of the sample in the Key is SeriesMeta::keyPen of the outline. The hatches are textures at two pixels per pixel, so that a figure at twice the size of the window draws them sharp. fill is null for a blocking time of no width.

◆ trainColourPlan()

TrainColourPlan trainColourPlan ( const QVector< TrainColourInput > & trains,
const QHash< QString, QColor > & serviceColours,
const QString & selectedTrain = QString() )

The colour rule of the diagram windows, by the trains shown. With six shown trains or fewer each train takes a colour of the palette #2a78d6, #c2448a, #4c8a12, #6127b1, #e35b6b, #842a65; with more shown trains in six services or fewer each service takes one; otherwise every shown train is grey, #8a97a1, and the selected train takes #2a78d6. A train whose service has a colour in the case, in serviceColours by train id (trainColorsForRun), takes that colour wherever the rule gives it one of the palette. The palette goes in the order of trains: by the place of a train, or of a service, among all the trains of the window while there are six of them or fewer, so that showing and hiding moves no colour, and otherwise among the shown ones. A hidden train is light grey, #c3cbd1, the colour of a hidden row in the Trains list. A train without a service is a service of its own. A copy takes the colour of the train it copies, or light grey while it is hidden, and is counted as that train, which the rule then counts as shown while the train or a copy of it is shown; selecting a copy selects its train. The ids of trains are distinct, and a train that a copy names is no copy.
