Detailed Description

What one train reuses within the steps of a run: the results of Train::BrakDist_Block of the current step and of the step before, and the route positions of its stops. A braking result depends on the arguments, on the rolling stock values of the train and on the arcs of the sections (their X, gradient and curvature), which only the builders write. A route position depends on the route index and the X of the stop, which are part of its key, and on the route, the stops and the stations of the run, which only the builders write. The cache answers only between beginStep and endStep, which runSimulationStep calls for every train around each step. It keeps the braking results of a step only for the step that follows it, and the route positions while each step follows the one before. A copy starts empty and outside a step, so a result never passes to another train or run.
