The unit normal (dy, -dx) of the segment of a preview line that covers the position rawX in kilometres, where (dx, dy) is the direction of increasing position in scene coordinates. A position at a bend takes the segment that leaves the bend. Returns false when no segment of nonzero length covers rawX.
