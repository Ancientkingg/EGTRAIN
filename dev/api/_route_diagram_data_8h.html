◆ delayDiagramData()

std::optional< DelayDiagramData > delayDiagramData ( const CompletedRunData & data,
int referenceIndex )

The arrival delay of each call of each train at a station of the reference route with the given index, at the position of that station on the route. A call is at the station nodes of the route whose station name is the station of the call (TimetableResultRow::stationId holds the name). When the route passes the station at more than one position, the call takes the first that the train reaches at or after its previous call at a station of the route, moving as between its last two such calls that lie apart, or before those as from its first to its last call at a station that the route passes once. Otherwise it takes the first of them on the route. Empty when the route does not exist or has no nodes.
