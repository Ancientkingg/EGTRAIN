Detailed Description

A signal head: the lamp of one direction of a signal, in rect in scene coordinates. Its colour, and a mark for any state but Proceed, show the state of the aspect code. A transparent child of 12 by 12 logical pixels at its centre keeps it clickable when the head is smaller on screen.

◆ inspectionIdentity()

QString SignalItem::inspectionIdentity ( ) const

That text, or without one a text of the track line, the position in kilometres and the sections ahead and behind: "Track B0 at 0.23 km; ahead B0 0.230 to 0.310 km; behind B0 0.000 to 0.230 km". The track is named by sceneTrackId and a section by its label, or by the number and the id without them.
