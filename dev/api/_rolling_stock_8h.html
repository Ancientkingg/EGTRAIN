The rule that finds the start of the approach to a block section at a fixed-block level.

Enumerator
Conventional 

Level 0: the approach starts when the head enters the section before.

Etcs2 

Level 2: the approach starts when the braking distance of the train first reaches the start of the section.

◆ buildOperationsFromScene()

std::vector< SceneDiagnostic > buildOperationsFromScene ( SimulationRun & run,
const SceneModel & scene,
const std::string & selectedScenarioId = {},
const SceneRunSelection & selectedOccurrences = {} )

Builds the trains, the timetable, the incidents and entrance delays of the selected scenario, the passenger capacity of the platforms and the passengers of a loaded scene into the run. selectedScenarioId and selectedOccurrences are those of DispatchController::prepareScene. The scene must pass validateRunnableScene: the builder does not repeat its checks of the platforms. The run's random generator is seeded from initial_variables.randomSeed first. When the diagnostics hold an error, nothing else of the run and nothing of initial_variables changes; otherwise initial_variables takes the name, base time, duration, buffer time, running time recovery and the numbers of tracks and routes of the scene.
