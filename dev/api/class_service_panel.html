Detailed Description

The services of the scene: a list to pick one and three tabs. The first holds the fields of the selected service, the second the place for its timetable, and the third lists the occurrences the services generate and lets the user leave some out of the next run. Renaming a service rewrites the entities that refer to it, and deleting one that is referred to is refused. The entry time and the headway are whole seconds, the entry time counted from the base time; the performance is in percent and the maximum speed in km/h.
