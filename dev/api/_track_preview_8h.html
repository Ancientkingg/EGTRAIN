◆ trackPreviewChainageCourse()

std::vector< TrackPreviewPoint > trackPreviewChainageCourse ( const TrackPreviewLine & line,
double fromKm,
double toKm )

The course of the line from chainage fromKm to toKm in kilometres, in the order of the line: the points at both chainages and the points of the line between them, with the display offset of the line added to y. A range that reaches past the drawn line, which for a double switch ends at its second and its last but one point, is cut at its ends. Empty when the range does not meet the drawn line or is not finite.

◆ trackPreviewSectionCourse()

std::vector< TrackPreviewPoint > trackPreviewSectionCourse ( const TrackPreviewResult & preview,
const SceneSectionDescriptor & section )

The course of a section on the preview, the points along which the track of the section is drawn: the point at its start chainage, the points of its nodes in order (SceneSectionDescriptor::nodeIds) and the point at its end chainage, each on the line of its own track, with the display offset of that line added to y. An end at a node adds no point of its own. A switch section goes from the line of its first track to the line of its second track at its connection. A double switch is drawn without its first and last arc, so the points on those arcs are left out. Empty when a node or an end of the section is on no line of the preview.
