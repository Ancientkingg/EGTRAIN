24// The infrastructure of the scene: a combo on top to pick the kind of entity, a sentence about the kind
25// (infrastructureGuidanceText), a table with one row for each entity of that kind, and under the table the buttons that
26// add and delete one. The kinds are the facets: tracks, nodes, arcs, blocks, connections, stations, platforms, signals,
27// signalling areas, routes, block dependencies, single-track restrictions and station boundaries. What a kind lists and
28// how it is edited is up to its InfrastructureFacet; a kind without one lists nothing. Blocks come with a filter on the
29// track under the combo and buttons that insert and move a block. Routes come with the heading "Route sections" and the
30// list of their sections and the buttons that change it, below the table in a splitter. The buttons call the facet that
31// is chosen.
