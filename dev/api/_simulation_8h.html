◆ Simulate_Train_Passenger_Interactions()

void Simulate_Train_Passenger_Interactions ( SimulationRun & run,
int t,
int SimulationStartingTime,
Train & T,
std::list< Passenger > & ALLPAX,
const std::list< StationPlatform > & ALLPLATFORMS )

At a service stop of train T in step t: the passengers whose trip ends there alight and transfer or leave the network, then the passengers waiting for T board in the order of the platform while T has room. In the first step of the stop, the passenger-dependent dwell replaces the stop time when it is longer. Passenger times are t + SimulationStartingTime (s since midnight).
