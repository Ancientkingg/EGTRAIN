◆ compressedBlockingTimeDiagramData()

std::optional< BlockingTimeDiagramData > compressedBlockingTimeDiagramData ( const CompletedRunData & data,
const CapacityAnalysisScope & scope,
const CapacityAnalysisResult & result )

The compressed blocking-time diagram of a capacity result, on the route and the blocks of its scope: the compressed occupations of result as envelopes, and the recorded samples of each train of the result that lie inside them, moved in time as its occupations are (compressed minus profile reference). It has no planned references. The time range spans the envelopes with a margin of 5 % of their span, at least 1 s, and starts below 0 s only when an envelope does; it is 0 s to 1 s without an envelope. Empty when the scope has no route.
